# REAPER COMPANION
## Master Roadmap

---

# Vision

Reaper Companion is not intended to be another Dead by Daylight database.

It is a decision engine that helps players make smarter choices before and during every match.

The goal is to remove guesswork from one of the most complex multiplayer games available.

Instead of overwhelming players with hundreds of perks, items, and add-ons, Reaper Companion recommends what they should use based on their objective.

---

# Mission

Help every Dead by Daylight player become a better player.

Whether they are new to the game or have thousands of hours, Reaper Companion should make choosing perks, builds, items, and strategies simple.

---

# Core Principles

Every feature should answer one question:

"What should I do?"

Not:

"What information exists?"

Reaper Companion should recommend rather than simply list.

Coaching is more valuable than encyclopedias.

Simple decisions beat complicated menus.

Examples:

• Blind Killer

• Heal Faster

• Best Perks

# Version 1.2

Expanded Match Coach

After the match begins:

Player selects the Killer.

Reaper Companion provides:

• Killer tips

• Counterplay

• Common mistakes

• Recommended priorities

• Looping advice

This is tactical guidance rather than loadout selection.

# Long-Term Goal

Players should recommend it because it helps them make better decisions, not because it contains the most data.

# Design Philosophy

Fast.

Professional.

Dark theme.

Minimal taps.

Every screen should have a purpose.

---

# Guiding Question

Whenever adding a feature ask:

Does this help the player make a better decision?

If the answer is yes—

Build it.

If not—

Leave it out.
